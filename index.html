Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026
In short: this app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
What this app does not do
  • No sign-up, login, or user accounts for attendees.
  • No collection of your name, email, phone number, or any other personal identifier.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no analytics SDKs that profile individual users.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • No sharing or selling of any data to third parties.
What the app does collect, and why
Location (optional, only if you grant permission). Used only to show your position on the in-app campus map and calculate walking directions to a building you search for. Your location is never stored on our servers, never logged, and never shared. It is used live, on your device, for the map feature only, and is discarded as soon as you leave the Campus Map screen.
Push notification token (optional, only if you grant permission). If you allow notifications, your device is assigned an anonymous push token (a random identifier with no link to your name or identity) so the conference organizers can send you live announcements, such as a session going live. This token cannot be used to identify you personally.
Admin accounts: a small number (1-3) of conference organizers have email/password accounts to post announcements. This policy does not apply to them as users of the app in the same way. Their accounts are provisioned manually by the organizers for operational purposes, not by public sign-up.
Data retention

Push tokens and any cached data are tied to this conference's database, which is deactivated after the conference concludes. This app has a planned one-month operational lifespan; see the organizers if you have questions about this timeline.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for conference attendees (academics, researchers, students) and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Questions about this policy or the app can be directed to the Department of English, Daffodil International University, via the contact details listed in the app's Contact screen or the official conference website.
